Pros of Agile Development:
- Allows for high flexibility of ideas and enables quick adoption of new projects
- There is constnat communication with the client, allowing for feedback and more desirable results
Cons of Agile Development:
- Difficult to make a plan for as it is constantly changing
- May be difficult to coordinate with other co-workers on a project


A Scrum Master is a person that focuses on collaboration between the team members, trying to make sure everyone is on the same page of a project. A Project Owner is someone who is responsible for the project's vision and prioritizing different features to maximize the value for the customer. Additionally, they act as a link between the customer and the project team.
