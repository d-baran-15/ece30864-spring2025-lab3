Pros of Agile Development:
- Allows for high flexibility of ideas and enables quick adoption of new projects
- There is constnat communication with the client, allowing for feedback and more desirable results
Cons of Agile Development:
- Difficult to make a plan for as it is constantly changing
- May be difficult to coordinate with other co-workers on a project


A Scrum Master is a person that focuses on collaboration between the team members, trying to make sure everyone is on the same page of a project. A Project Owner is someone who is responsible for the project's vision and prioritizing different features to maximize the value for the customer. Additionally, they act as a link between the customer and the project team.


The three types of meetings that happen during a sprint cycle are a planning meeting, a standup meeting, and a retrospective meeting. In a planning meeting, the team needs o determine what to complete in the coming sprint as well as determine story points and assign stories to their appropriate teams. In a standup meeting, team members discuss work completed as well as what the plan for that day is. In a retrospective meeting, the team discusses the work that has been completed and move incommplete work to the backlog.


In JIRA, an Epic captures a large body of work that can be broken down into several smaller stories. A story is a project requirement that has a short description. A bug is an issue within the project and often leads to other parts of the project being blocked. A task is a unit of work that needs to be completed to finish a story.
